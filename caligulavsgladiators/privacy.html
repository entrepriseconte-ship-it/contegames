Privacy policy – Caligula vs Gladiators

Updated: October 13, 2026. Publisher: Conte Games Studio.

In short

No account, no email. The game is free thanks to ads from Google AdMob and its partners: personalized ads and tracking are only turned on with your consent, never under 16. The game is playable offline (and then shows no ads).

On your device

The game saves your progress (arenas, stars, coins, outfits), your language and the sound setting. This data stays on your device.

World leaderboard (optional)

Only if you choose a gladiator name, the game sends to our server (hosted by Cloudflare): a random identifier (not your device's), your gladiator name, your glory score (stars and coins) and the device type (iOS, Android, PC). Your country is derived from the connection. Your IP address is not stored with your score: it is only used to block abusive submissions and is erased within an hour. Your name and score are visible to other players.

Duel mode (optional)

Only if you have chosen a gladiator name, ranked Duel mode also sends to the same server your level, your equipment and your combat power, to find opponents of similar strength, then the result and length of each duel, to prevent rigged fights. Your name, country, level and equipment may appear to other players as a computer-controlled "ghost" gladiator. No identifier of your device is sent. In live duels, your fight controls go through the server to the other player and are not kept.

Ads

The game shows an ad screen before some arenas (never two within 2 minutes) and offers, if you choose, a video to return to the fight. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, with Unity Ads and AppLovin as partners. To choose and show ads, these services may receive: your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow it), your IP address, an approximate location derived from the connection, the device model and your interactions with ads. In the European Union, a Google form asks for your consent at first launch; on iPhone, Apple asks whether you allow tracking (only from age 16). Without consent, ads stay non-personalized. You can change your mind at any time in Settings > Privacy and ads, and on iPhone in Settings > Privacy > Tracking. Partner policies: policies.google.com/privacy, unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy, applovin.com/privacy. Full policy: contegames.com/caligulavsgladiators/confidentialite.html

Technical services

The game is made with Unity, which may collect anonymous technical data (crashes, performance) under its own policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. The App Store and Google Play also apply their own rules.

Children

At first launch, the game asks for your year of birth, kept on your device, to adapt the ads: under 13, only child-appropriate ads; under 16, no tracking and no personalized ads. The game never asks for your real name, email, photos or precise location.

Your rights

Settings > Delete my data erases everything on your device and removes your entry from the world leaderboard. For any question, write to the developer email shown on the game's App Store or Google Play page.

No sale of data

We do not sell or rent any data. It is only shared with the leaderboard host and, for ads, with Google AdMob and its partners, as described above.
