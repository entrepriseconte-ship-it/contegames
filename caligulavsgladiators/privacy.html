Caligula vs Gladiators Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · CONTE GAMES STUDIO

In short

  • Caligula vs Gladiators is a game. There is no account to create and the publisher has no server that receives your data: your progress, coins, gear and year of birth stay on your phone.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob and its partners Unity Ads and AppLovin. They then receive some technical data (for example your IP address and device identifiers).
  • On first launch, the game asks for your year of birth to adapt ads to your age: under 16, no personalised ads.
  • On iPhone, from age 16, the game may ask for Apple's tracking permission: you can refuse and the game stays the same.
  • The game offers no purchases.
  • Any questions: contegamesstudio@gmail.com.

1. Who is responsible?

Caligula vs Gladiators is published by Anthony Conte, sole trader (French « entrepreneur individuel »), trading as CONTE GAMES STUDIO (SIRET 490 066 602 00056), 42 C rue du Général de Gaulle, 42400 Saint-Chamond, France. Contact: contegamesstudio@gmail.com.

The publisher is the "controller" within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for its own part. Google, Unity, AppLovin and Apple decide some processing themselves: they are then responsible for it, under their own policies (see section 3).

2. Data that stays on your phone

This data is stored in a save file on your device. It is not sent to the publisher:

  • your progress (arenas won, records), coins and gear;
  • your year of birth (see section 6);
  • your settings (language, sound, music);
  • your privacy choices (stored by Google's consent tool).

The "Delete my data" button in Settings erases it. If you uninstall the game, this data is also deleted from your device. The game has no online save: progress cannot be recovered afterwards.

3. Data processed by our partners

3.1 Ads: Google AdMob, Unity Ads and AppLovin

The game shows an ad before each arena, and offers a rewarded ad when your gladiator falls: if you choose to watch it, he gets back into the fight with a quarter of his health. This one always stays optional. Only ads suitable for the game's age rating are requested.

To do so, Google's Mobile Ads SDK is built into the game. According to Google, it may collect: your IP address (which gives an approximate location, not a precise address), device identifiers (advertising ID, other app-related identifiers), interactions (app launches, taps, ads viewed) and diagnostic and performance data. This data is used to show ads, measure their effectiveness, produce statistics and prevent fraud. It is encrypted in transit.

For each ad, Google AdMob picks the best ad partner ("mediation"): Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and AppLovin may then receive the same kind of data to show their ad. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and AppLovin's Privacy Policy.

  • Personalised ads: only for players aged 16 or over who accept them in the consent message shown on first launch (in Europe, the UK and Switzerland).
  • If you refuse, or if you are under 16: Google shows non-personalised or limited ads. The game stays exactly the same.
  • On iPhone, if you are 16 or over, the game then shows Apple's tracking permission window. If you allow it, Apple's advertising identifier may be used to personalise ads and measure them across apps. If you refuse, it is not used. You can change your mind at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Google processes this data under its own rules, as a controller for its own part: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3.2 Game engine: Unity

The game is built with the Unity engine. No Unity analytics service (Unity Analytics, crash reporting) is enabled in the game.

5. Your choices and how to withdraw consent

  • In the game: Settings > "PRIVACY & ADS" reopens Google's consent message so you can accept or refuse again (this button appears where the law of your country requires it).
  • On Android: in your phone settings, under Google ads, you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • On iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, to allow or refuse tracking by the game.

Withdrawing consent changes nothing in the game and does not affect what was done before.

6. Children and teenagers

Caligula vs Gladiators is an arcade game recommended from age 9. On first launch, the game asks for the player's year of birth, without suggesting an answer and without saying what the answer changes. This information stays on the phone. As the day of birth is not asked, the game always assumes the youngest possible age.

  • Under 13: ad requests are marked as directed to a child; only non-personalised ads suitable for all ages are shown; no consent request is shown.
  • 13 to 15: in France, a minor under 15 cannot consent alone to the processing of their data for this kind of service, and other European countries set this age up to 16. The game therefore only shows non-personalised ads until 16, with no consent or tracking request.
  • The publisher does not collect personal data from children. If you think a child has sent us data (for example by email), write to us and we will delete it.

7. How long

  • Data on your phone: until the game is uninstalled.
  • Data processed by Google, Unity, AppLovin and Apple: under their own policies (links above).
  • Your messages to the publisher: 3 years after the last exchange.

8. Recipients and transfers outside the European Union

The data described in section 3 is processed by Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC), Unity Technologies, AppLovin and Apple. Some of it may be transferred outside the European Union, in particular to the United States. These companies state that they cover such transfers with the safeguards provided by the GDPR (for example the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework): see their privacy policies.

The publisher does not sell your data.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict its use, object to it, receive it (portability), withdraw your consent and give instructions about it after your death. As the publisher holds almost no data about you, most of these requests are made directly to Google, Unity, AppLovin or Apple (links in section 3); the publisher can help you.

Contact: contegamesstudio@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or with the authority of your country.

10. Security

Data sent by our partners is encrypted in transit. Game data stays on your device, protected by your own settings (passcode, fingerprint, etc.).

11. Changes

If this policy changes (for example when an online leaderboard arrives), the new version will be published at the same address with its date, and announced in the game if the change is significant.
