Caligula vs Gladiators Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · CONTE GAMES STUDIO

In short

  • There is no account to create.
  • The publisher has no server that receives your data. The game offers no purchases.
  • The game shows non-personalised ads through Unity Ads: one before each arena, and an optional one to get back into the fight after a defeat. Unity then receives some technical data (for example your IP address and device identifiers).
  • The game does not ask for Apple's tracking permission and does not use the advertising identifier.
  • Your progress and your gladiator's name are stored only on your phone.
  • Any questions: contegamesstudio@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your phone

To work, the game stores in a save file on your device:

  • your progress (arenas won, coins, equipment, records);
  • your gladiator's name;
  • your settings (language, sound, music).

This data is not sent to the publisher. The "Delete my data" button in Settings erases it. Uninstalling the game also erases it: progress cannot be recovered.

3. Ads: Unity Ads

The game shows an ad before each arena, and offers a rewarded ad when your gladiator falls: if you choose to watch it, he gets back into the fight with a quarter of his health. You can always decline this one.

To do this, the Unity Ads kit from Unity Technologies is built into the game. According to Unity, it may collect: your IP address (which gives an approximate location, not a precise address), app-related device identifiers, device information, ad interactions and diagnostic data. This data is used to show ads, measure their display and fight fraud. It is encrypted in transit.

Non-personalised ads for everyone: the game asks Unity not to use your data to show you targeted ads. On iPhone, the game does not ask for Apple's tracking permission: Apple's advertising identifier is not used.

Unity processes this data under its own rules, as a controller for its own part: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Some data may be transferred outside the European Union, notably to the United States, with the safeguards provided by the GDPR.

3a. What the publisher does not do

  • The publisher does not collect, sell or share any personal data of players.
  • The game does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • The game offers no in-app purchases.

4. Children

The game is not specifically aimed at children. Ads are always non-personalised. The publisher collects no personal data, including children's data. If you believe a child has sent us information (for example by email), write to us and we will delete it.

5. Your rights

As the publisher holds no data about you, there is nothing to correct or delete on its side; requests about ad data go to Unity (link in section 3), and the publisher can help you. You can still write to us about your personal data: we answer within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr.

6. Changes

If a future version of the game changes these rules (for example with an online leaderboard, personalised ads or purchases), this policy will be updated at the same address, with its date, before that version is released.
