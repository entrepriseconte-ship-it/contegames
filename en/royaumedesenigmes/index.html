Riddles and matches to save the kingdom

In Royaume des Énigmes, the queen needs your help. Every level is a race against time: make matches of symbols on the board and answer riddles to win extra time before the hourglass runs out.

How do you play?

The adventure includes 30 levels, 3 fearsome bosses and 6 bonus games. Victories earn coins and relics: gather all 50 relics of the chest to receive the Eternal Blessing.

When can I download it?

Royaume des Énigmes is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and to Android. The download button will appear at the top of this page as soon as it is released.
