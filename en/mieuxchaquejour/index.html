Yoga, nutrition and habits: Mieux Chaque Jour on iPhone

Mieux Chaque Jour is a well-being app available on iPhone, designed to bring several everyday activities together in one place: yoga exercises, guidance on food and nutrition, and simple habits, with a gradual approach rather than a single goal to reach.

What's inside the app?

Three themes: moving with step-by-step guided exercises, better understanding your food choices with a guide to 1,500 foods and 1,000 recipes, and keeping track of small well-being habits throughout the day, at your own pace.

Who is it for?

It may appeal to people who want to take time for themselves, discover yoga or build a personal routine. Well-being information is not a substitute for medical advice or support suited to a specific health situation.

How do I download it?

Mieux Chaque Jour is available now on the App Store for iPhone: tap the App Store button at the top of this page to download it. The Android version is planned for November 15, 2026.
