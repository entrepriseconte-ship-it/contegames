Our mobile game studio

Conte Games Studio is an independent mobile game studio, born in Saint-Chamond (Loire, France). It was founded by Anthony Conte, who imagines, draws and publishes the games with one idea: free games, easy to play and made to be shared with the family.

The studio website is available in 130 languages, so every player can find the games in their own language.

Frequently asked questions

Are Conte Games Studio games free?

Yes, the studio’s games can be downloaded for free. Each game page gives the details.

Where can I download the games?

On the App Store for iPhone and, for some games, on Google Play for Android. Each game page shows the official buttons once the game is available.

Who is behind the studio?

Anthony Conte, a sole proprietor based in Saint-Chamond, France.

How can I contact the studio?

By e-mail at contegamesstudio@gmail.com.
