Royaume des Mots CroisésThe Crossword Kingdom

Solve crossword puzzles and build your own kingdom.

Download on theApp StoreComing to Android on November 5, 2026

Crossword puzzles meet a growing medieval kingdom

Royaume des Mots Croisés combines word puzzles with the pleasure of developing a medieval village. Solving grids is part of a larger adventure: each successful challenge helps you move towards building a kingdom, one small step at a time.

How does progression work?

The planned gameplay revolves around solving crossword grids, earning ducats and using those rewards to grow your village. It brings together vocabulary puzzles and a kingdom-building theme, giving players a reason to return to the next grid.

Will an account be required?

The project currently plans to keep your progress on your phone without requiring an account. These details describe a game in development; features may change before release.

When can I download it?

Royaume des Mots Croisés is available now on the App Store for iPhone: tap the App Store button at the top of this page to download it.
