Konzaninso nyumba yachifumu yamatsenga, puzzle imodzi imodzi

Les Tableaux Ensorcelés ndi masewera aulere a puzzle opezeka pa Android / iPhone ndi iPad. Lowani m'nyumba yachifumu yankhani zongoyerekeza yodzaza ndi zithunzi zomwe zikudikira kukonzedwanso. Nkhaniyi ikutsatira Maître Léonard pamene nyumbayo ikukumana ndi ndewu za Barbouille ndi Malgrimoire.

Kodi pali mapuzzle amtundu wanji?

Masewerawa ali ndi zithunzi zamatsenga 50 zokhala ndi zovuta kuyambira zidutswa 9 mpaka 100. Amapereka mitundu iwiri ya puzzle: mapuzzle otsetsereka ndi mapuzzle achikhalidwe ngati jigsaw, kulola osewera kuyandikira zojambulazo m'njira zosiyanasiyana.

Kodi pali zina zotani zoti mudziwe?

Kupatula zithunzizo, masewerawa ali ndi nyumba yowonetsera ya zipinda zisanu yoti mukonzenso ndi kukongoletsa, puzzle ya tsiku ndi tsiku, zovuta za mlungu uliwonse, mphotho ndi Royal Duel ya mphindi zisanu. Zinthu zingasinthe ndi zosintha zamtsogolo.

Kodi masewerawa ndi aulere?

Les Tableaux Ensorcelés ndi yaulere komanso yopanda zotsatsa pa iPhone ndi iPad. Itsitseni tsopano pogwiritsa ntchito batani la App Store lili pamwambapa.
